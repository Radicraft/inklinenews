Yes. The BBC maintains one of the most extensive free feed sets of any news organisation, and it was working at the registry pass of 17 July 2026.

Provision covers the main news front page, the major desks including business, politics, technology, science and health, the UK nations and English regions, and sport. Everything is free to read in full, and updates arrive quickly around breaking stories. The set has thinned over the years at the margins, and some programme-level feeds have quietly gone, which is why a dated check matters more than an old bookmark.

The practical catch is volume. The BBC publishes at a rate that will bury a casual feed list, so the useful setup follows the desks you actually need rather than the firehose. The [BBC News page in our directory](/publications/bbc-news/) records its coverage and access status, free across the set.

For monitoring use, the BBC pairs well with a wire and a specialist title: the BBC for reach and speed, [Reuters](/publications/reuters/) for the first version of events, and your sector's trade press for the depth.
